About Us

Why Work With Us?

53+ Years of Impact

Impact Consulting Group, originally formed in 1972, is an MBA student-led management consulting firm based out of the Rotman School of Management at the University of Toronto.

We offer innovative and high-quality tailored solutions that facilitate our clients' growth. Impact's perspectives are infused with the latest education, diverse industry and functional experience, and the resources of Canada's leading business school.

With over 20 years of combined experience, we leverage the expertise of the faculty to capitalize on the state-of-the-art business information that provides clients with a unique, yet practical approach.
